Nov 18 , 12:10:00 GMT+0 Resolved - During [Cloudflare's 2025-11-18 incident](https://blog.cloudflare.com/18-november-2025-outage/), loading of Device Fingerprinting's frontend script was inaccessible for customers using a custom domain powered by Cloudflare. Any client that normally would have been fingerprinted during this time would have failed to do so and received no telemetry.
